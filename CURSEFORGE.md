## Summary

Lists the players around you who are missing your buffs, your group first. Click the top line, or
press one key, to buff them one after another.

## Description

Buffs last an hour on Forever, so buffing people in town is worth it. The hard part is knowing who
still needs one, and Serial Buffer keeps that list. A small panel shows every friendly player in
range who's missing one of your buffs, your group first. Click a line and that player gets the buff
and drops off the list. Or bind "Next buff" in the game's key bindings and just keep pressing it.

- Mages, priests, druids, warlocks and shamans hand out all their buffs with no setup.
- Paladins pick a first, second and third blessing per class in the options (Kings, Wisdom, Might
  by default), and skip what another paladin already cast.
- Salvation gets its own lines when your group has a tank: everyone gets it but the tanks, and a
  tank wearing it gets a Revoke line.
- 10-minute buffs like Thorns stay in your group unless you tick "For everyone".

In combat the panel freezes, but the key keeps going down your group, one player per press. In
battlegrounds the game hides buffs, so the list follows your own casts instead.

You need friendly nameplates on (Options, Nameplates). In dungeons, only your group is listed.
/sbuff shows or hides the panel, /sbuff options opens the settings. English, French, German, Spanish.
